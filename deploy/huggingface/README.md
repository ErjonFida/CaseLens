# CaseLens

Question answering over legal contracts, with retrieval and answers measured
against expert annotations. Press **Try the demo** on the sign-in page: five
contracts from [CUAD](https://www.atticusprojectai.org/cuad) (CC BY 4.0),
read-only, no account needed.

Source, evaluation and write-up: https://github.com/ErjonFida/CaseLens
